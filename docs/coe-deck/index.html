02 · The proposal in one line

BGR Neo becomes a Centre of Excellence awarding a real postgraduate qualification in AI for robotics — and a technician trade for ITI passouts.

Recognised

A degree, not a certificate

A UGC Postgraduate Diploma — 40 credits — carried on the graduate's academic record, recognised by employers, by government schemes and by universities for further study. Not a certificate of attendance.

Anchored

On four brands, not one

FANUC, ABB, KUKA and Universal Robots in one room, taught by engineers who commission cells for a living. An OEM-funded centre cannot match this without losing its funding.

Independent

Not waiting on a scheme

The award comes from Loyola College, autonomous under UGC. It needs no government scheme to exist, and no committee has to pick us first. Government partnership is pursued alongside it, not depended on.

Scope is all types of AI, not only the AI inside a robot: the software agents that assist engineers, and the analytics that predict failures and defects, matter as much on a factory floor as machine vision does.

03 · Why now

The ecosystem already exists. We plug into it — we don't invent it.

The people

Trained in volume, placed unmeasured

Tamil Nadu graduates engineers in volume and now trains them on robots too — 71 government ITIs, ₹2,877 crore. Those centres publish admission numbers and no placement outcomes whatsoever. The gap is not exposure any more. It is whether anyone can prove the training worked.

The skill gap

AI and robotics, together

Factories are adding vision, predictive maintenance and AI-assisted programming. The people who can do both sides — the robot and the AI — barely exist.

The push

Government is already funding this

Central and state skilling bodies are actively standing up centres for exactly AI, robotics and advanced manufacturing. The money and the mechanism are live now.

Central · MSDE

National Centres of Excellence for Skilling

Launched targeting AI, robotics and advanced manufacturing, delivering nationally-aligned training at NSQF levels ~5–8, operationalised through National Skill Training Institutes.

State · TNSDC

A relationship to build, not a door to walk through

Tamil Nadu's nodal skilling agency funds industry-run skilling facilities. But its last published CoE Expression of Interest closed in January 2025, and never covered robotics or manufacturing. There is no open call to answer today — which is exactly why the award should not depend on one.

State · Naan Mudhalvan

Reach into every college

The Chief Minister's flagship embeds industry courses into engineering curricula as mandatory credit, via train-the-trainer, targeting 10+ lakh students. Our future feeder.

05 · Our recommendation

Award the qualification through Loyola now. Build the government relationship alongside it — not in front of it.

Reason 01

It is the only model that carries an award

A training-partner contract or an in-college module hands out a certificate. Only a Centre of Excellence with a university partner can hand a graduate a postgraduate qualification — and that is the difference an employer and a funding scheme both recognise.

Reason 02

We control the critical path

A UGC-recognised diploma through an autonomous college depends on Loyola's Academic Council, not on a state committee's calendar. The government route is real and worth building, but its last robotics-relevant door closed in January 2025 — we should not stake the launch date on the next one opening.

Reason 03

It keeps the bigger plays open

Starting at state level does not close the national route or full ownership. It earns the track record that makes both easier to win later.

The template for later

TNASDCL

Tamil Nadu's Apex Skill Development Centre for Logistics — a Section 8 not-for-profit formed as a partnership between TNSDC, a sector skill council and industry (TVS). Precisely the structure a BGR Neo skilling company would take.

Proof it can earn

TN Semiconductor Mission CoEs

Established as for-profit special-purpose vehicles with academia and industry. A Centre of Excellence does not have to be a cost centre — there is a live precedent for it being a business.

08 · Eligibility — the raw-material spec

We test what a candidate can do, not which degree they hold.

The gate

A short entrance assessment

Basic programming and mathematics. Freshers show projects or internships. Not a degree wall.

Direct fit
Computer Science · ECE / EEE · Mechatronics · Robotics & Automation · Instrumentation · Mechanical. Enter the programme directly.
Adjacent, with bridging
Physics, Mathematics, and other engineering disciplines with programming ability. Enter after a short bridge module.
Needs major bridging
Everyone else. Routed to foundation preparation first — not turned away, sequenced.
Why a soft gate, deliberately: a government programme is judged on employability and inclusion. A "direct-fit degrees only" filter would work against the very goals the scheme is funded to achieve — and would quietly exclude capable candidates. The assessment protects quality; the bands protect access.

Entry is mapped to national qualification levels so the certificate a student earns is recognised anywhere in India, not just inside BGR Neo.

  • Foundation, everyone: maths and programming · software engineering · ROS 2, kinematics and control · machine learning · computer vision · robot learning · LLMs, retrieval and agents
  • Then one of five: Industrial Manufacturing · Perception & Autonomy · Robot Learning · Factory Analytics · Agentic & Applied AI
  • Only one of the five leads to a factory. The others lead to defence, drones, autonomous vehicles, marine and agricultural robotics
Technician · 8 months · 30 students

Automation Uptime Technician

1,215 hours over 32 weeks for ITI passouts, certified in two separate halves so a trainee who leaves at week 16 still leaves with a qualification.

  • Week 16 — Automation Cell Operator. Runs any cell, any robot brand. Employable here
  • Week 32 — Automation Uptime Technician. Diagnoses and repairs across every subsystem. This is the pay step
  • Four robot brands take 140 hours of it; a 48-scenario fault library takes 90 more, with recovery time measured and graded

A third programme — a 6-month engineer training course cut from the diploma's own module library — is designed and held back. It shares the same lab entirely, so it can be added in any later year without buying anything.

Where the money goes: the live cell hall is 36% of it, the computer lab 16%. Adding all four non-manufacturing specialisations costs ₹0.6–1.1 crore, because 30 students split five ways is about six per track — and two of the five need no new hardware at all.

04 · Credibility

The track record for bigger plays

A successful state CoE is the evidence base for a national centre, or for our own skilling company.

Research found that the automation council publishes no robotics qualification pack at all, and the two candidate national qualifications both set a minimum job-entry age of 24 — which excludes a fresh-graduate cohort finishing at 22. The university award is not a fallback; it is the only route that works today. Sponsoring our own national qualification is the year-two moat — a state skill corporation has done exactly that before.

14 · Roadmap

Get the award approved. Prove it on one cohort. Then scale, and sponsor our own qualification.

Phase 0

Prepare

Loyola’s Board of Studies and Academic Council: the syllabus, credit map and examination scheme. Three vendor quotations in parallel.

Phase 1

Win the EOI

Approve the award, sign the lab, appoint the trainers. Open applications for 30 diploma and 30 technician places.

Phase 2

Pilot cohort

Foundation delivered in simulation; specialisation on one live cell. Measure placement, not attendance.

Phase 3

Scale

Add cells and cohorts. Turn on the college feeders — Naan Mudhalvan and training-partner routes.

Phase 4 · optional

Spin into a company

If the centre earns, structure it as its own entity — following the TNASDCL and semiconductor precedents.

Timing is an assumption, not a commitment. The pace is set by Loyola’s Academic Council cycle. That is a lead-time question we can ask this month. Phases 0 and 1 should be treated as this financial year's work; everything after depends on winning.
