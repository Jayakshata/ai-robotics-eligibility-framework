OverviewThe proposition

A government-partnered Centre of Excellence training two populations for the same factories: graduate engineers who build automated cells, and post-ITI technicians who keep them running. Both learn on cells that are doing paying customer work.

Anyone can put a trainee in front of a robot. Almost nobody can put one in front of a robot that has a customer, an acceptance test, and a consequence for downtime. The earlier version of this claim — that nobody in Tamil Nadu has real robots — was false. The state has already put an Industrial Robotics lab into 71 government ITIs. What those labs do not have is an order book.

What actually differentiates the Centre

  • A production cell, not a training cell. A live customer integration project with a real handover.
  • Multi-vendor. FANUC, ABB, KUKA and UR in one room, plus integration across PLC, vision, safety and fieldbus that no single OEM owns — structurally impossible for an OEM-run centre.
  • Uptime as the job definition. No programme found anywhere in India is built around a whole-cell fault taxonomy, a drilled diagnostic loop, seeded-fault assessment on an unseen cell, and time-to-recovery as a graded outcome.
  • Post-NTC conversion of Electrician and Fitter passouts — a population the large national skilling providers route away from automation.

OverviewWho awards what — and why it is Loyola College

AICTE sets a Post Graduate Diploma in Engineering & Technology at two years, and its one-year post-graduate engineering award is a Certificate. None of that binds Loyola College, which is an autonomous arts & science college under UGC — where a P.G. Diploma is a 40-credit, Level 6 award and an autonomous Academic Council approves its own curriculum. A one-year, 40-credit PG Diploma in Applied AI is ordinary business there.

The three Loyola entities are governed differently — this choice is load-bearing

Sequencing — reversed

Sign Loyola before or alongside any government filing, not after. The curriculum needs Academic Council approval on Loyola's calendar, and the TNSDC CoE route carries an explicit Knowledge Partner slot — so Loyola goes into the bid rather than being courted separately.

First question to ask them: will the Academic Council approve a programme where the 260-hour specialization block runs off-campus at a BGR Neo facility?

No QP claim for the 6-month engineer product

Both candidate Qualification Packs — one from the Capital Goods council, one from NASSCOM — set a minimum job-entry age of 24, and one also requires a degree plus two years' experience. A cohort of fresh graduates finishing at 22–23 is excluded from both. The interim credential is a CoE certificate plus vendor certificates, and the deck should say so plainly.

Why 450, not 380

The earlier 380 + 380 = 760 structure fitted no NSQF band at all. The Level 4 notional band is 390–480 hours, so 380 was ten hours below the floor; Level 5 requires three years' experience, which a fresh passout does not have; and 760 h exceeded every short-term band while falling far below the long-term norm.

450 h sits on the Level 4 band, equals 15 credits exactly, and lands inside the 300–600 h national short-term funding window — so each tier is independently fundable. A single 760-hour award was fundable by nothing.

Same level, scarcer competence. A fresh two-year-trade ITI passout already holds NSQF Level 4, so claiming a level jump would be caught. The honest claim is better: an Electrician at Level 4 and an automation uptime technician at Level 4 are not interchangeable on a plant floor.

Material is the domain every programme forgets. In palletizing and truck loading a large share of real stoppages are a crushed carton or a shifted load. A technician who assumes every fault is the machine's fault will burn hours.

The Six-Step Fault Loop — drilled to reflex, timed from day one

StepActionThe discipline
1 · Make safeStop state, isolate energy, LOTO before entering the cageNever troubleshoot a live cell you are standing inside
2 · Look & logRead the alarm and HMI, note the last good cycle — photograph before touchingThe evidence is destroyed the moment you start fixing
3 · LocateNarrow to one of the seven domains by halving the cellBinary search beats intuition
4 · Fix or flagRepair inside your authority, or escalate with a structured reportKnowing what you may not touch is the skill
5 · RestartControlled restart, verify good cycles, check first pieceAn unverified restart is a fault you meet again in an hour
6 · RecordCMMS entry: fault, cause, action, parts, downtime minutesAn unrecorded fault teaches the plant nothing

The authority boundary is printed with its AFNOR NF X 60-010 maintenance-level mapping, so a graduate is legible to the French-, German-, Japanese- and Korean-parented plants that dominate Tamil Nadu automation. Bypassing a safety device is an automatic fail in assessment and a dismissal offence on site.

The Handover Model — milestone-gated, not daily

Handovers occur at FAT, SAT and production-ramp boundaries — roughly 6–8 per cohort — plus daily shift-log handovers inside the technician cohort's own shifts. An earlier version claimed 120 daily handovers, which was arithmetically impossible, and required a cell under active integration to be production-ready every evening, which it cannot be.

It runs on a ring-fenced training cell, never a customer-acceptance cell, and every milestone carries a staffed ISO 10218 safety re-validation step. What survives is the good part: the engineers' handover module gets a real audience, the technicians' genuine faults become the engineers' debugging work, and both live the real arc of an integration project.

Four observations for the capital plan

  • The graduate tier needs no robots for its first half. Weeks 1–30 of the diploma and 1–14 of the training run on Group A compute and open-source simulation, so Group C can be staged.
  • The technician tier cannot be staged the same way — it needs trainer cells from week 3 — but what it needs early is the cheap kit, not the industrial cells.
  • The two tiers are complementary in capex, not competing. The graduate tier is compute-heavy and hands-light; the technician tier is the reverse and needs no GPU workstations at all.
  • The assessment cell is not a luxury. Without a cell no cohort trains on, the technician tier's headline examination — an unseen cell with three seeded faults — cannot be run as specified.
The capex story may invert

The published state CoE route provides the physical space "in any of the government institutions or building," along with furniture, workstation PCs and connectivity, while the partner supplies the specialist equipment. If the Centre is sited that way, "mostly buying computers, not robots" reverses exactly — Groups A and J shrink and Group C becomes the whole spend. Both scenarios need costing before the money slide is written.
