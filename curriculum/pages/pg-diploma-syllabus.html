Why exactly 1,200 hours. Unlike the technician programme, this figure is not free to choose. A Postgraduate Diploma is fixed at 40 credits, and the national credit framework sets 1 credit = 30 notional learning hours. 40 × 30 = 1,200. Notional hours include directed teaching and supervised self-study, so contact time is lower than the headline number.
