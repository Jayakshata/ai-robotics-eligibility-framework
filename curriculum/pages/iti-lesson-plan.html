Automation Uptime Technician

A full trade programme for ITI passouts. It puts a technician on any cell, any robot brand, any corner of the factory — and it is sized honestly for what that actually takes.

Why 1,215 hours. It is not a target — it is what the content costs. Four teach-pendant families take 140 hours on their own; the 48-scenario fault library takes 90; the two application rotations take 150; and PLC reading takes 80 because most entrants have never seen one. For reference, a national one-year ITI trade is 1,200 taught hours — so this is one trade-year of content, delivered in eight months. With the bridge, a Stream B entrant is in the centre for about 36 weeks.
Non-negotiable

Bypassing, defeating or jumpering a safety device is an automatic fail in every assessment, at either tier. Module T1-01 defines the three authority levels — do it · do it and report it · never do it — and the trainee carries that card for the whole programme.

574 ITIs run Electrician and Fitter, and neither teaches any automation at all. That is who you will mostly get. The 150-hour Stream B bridge exists for them, and it is not remedial — it is the content their trade never contained.

What "any cell, any corner" means

The claim is only true if the trainee has physically handled each of these. Every line below maps to a module that delivers it.
