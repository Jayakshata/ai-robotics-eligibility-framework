Since **2026-07-28** the [[bgr-neo-coe-deck]] work expanded from a deck into a full **curriculum workstream**, held in `curriculum/` on branch **`coe-curriculum`** (not `main`). Three programmes exist, each with per-submodule hours that reconcile exactly:

- **PG Diploma in Applied AI for Industrial Robotics** — 1,200 h / 48 weeks / **40 credits**. The 1,200 is not a free choice: a PG Diploma is fixed at 40 credits and NCrF sets 1 credit = 30 notional hours.
- **Engineer Training** — 810 h / 26 weeks, CoE certificate only (both candidate QPs age-gate at 24, so no NSQF claim).
- **Automation Uptime Technician (post-ITI)** — 1,215 h / 32 weeks, two NSQF Level 4 awards. Sized bottom-up from content rather than to fit a funding band, by explicit decision.

**Settled, do not relitigate:** the academic partner is **Loyola College, Chennai** — autonomous arts & science under **UGC**, *not* AICTE. That distinction is load-bearing: AICTE's two-year PG-Diploma rule binds only Engineering & Technology, so Loyola's own Academic Council can approve a one-year 40-credit diploma. LICET (AICTE, Anna-affiliated) would reimpose every constraint; LIBA is the relationship door into the Jesuit system, not the awarding body. Sign the partner **before or alongside** any government filing, via the EOI's Knowledge Partner slot.

**All four robot brands — FANUC, ABB, KUKA, Universal Robots — are non-negotiable.** That costs 140 h in the technician programme and is the one thing no single-brand OEM centre can copy.

**A 13-agent research pass with four adversarial reviewers (2026-07-29) returned `PLAN_NEEDS_CHANGE` on every dimension.** Findings with source URLs are in `curriculum/VALIDATION-REPORT.md`; the corrections are applied. Three must never be reintroduced: the old wedge ("nobody has real robots") is **false** — Tamil Nadu put robotics labs into 71 government ITIs at ₹2,877 cr; **IASC publishes no robotics QP** and its lists use colliding codes, so name no QP code until confirmed in writing; and the **TNSDC CoE EOI closed in Jan 2025** and never covered this sector.
